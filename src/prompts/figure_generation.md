# Textbook Diagram Generation Prompt

Generate a clean, minimal textbook-style educational diagram intended for STEM and engineering problems.

The image should function as a GENERAL conceptual representation of the problem rather than a highly detailed illustration. The goal is to visually support adaptive or parameterized questions without hardcoding specific numerical values, dimensions, or unnecessary contextual details.

## Visual Style Requirements

* Use a minimalist textbook line-art style
* Prioritize clarity and readability over realism
* Use thin, clean black outlines
* White or transparent background preferred
* Minimal shading or texture
* Avoid clutter and excessive environmental detail
* Keep the composition simple and instructional
* The diagram should resemble a generic physics/mechanics textbook illustration

## Educational Purpose

The image should:

* visually communicate the physical scenario
* help students understand geometry, motion, orientation, or setup
* remain reusable across multiple generated question variations
* avoid embedding fixed values or problem-specific numbers directly into the image

## Important Constraints

Do NOT:

* hardcode numerical values
* include unnecessary realism
* include detailed backgrounds
* include excessive facial detail
* include logos, branding, or decorative elements
* add large blocks of text
* make the diagram overly stylized or cinematic

The image should remain flexible enough to support dynamically generated educational questions.

## Labeling Guidelines

Use only generic symbolic labels when necessary, such as:

* $v_0$
* $\theta$
* $x$
* $y$
* $F$
* $m$
* $L$

Labels should:

* be sparse
* instructional
* mathematically clean
* not overcrowd the diagram

## Preferred Style Characteristics

Preferred aesthetic:

* introductory physics textbook
* engineering mechanics workbook
* exam/homework diagram
* clean vector-style educational graphic
* monochrome or near-monochrome line art

## Example Scenario

Example:
A football player throwing a football at an angle:

* show a simplified player silhouette
* dashed projectile trajectory
* launch angle marker
* optional coordinate axes
* no stadium
* no crowd
* no detailed textures
* no fixed measurements

The diagram should feel generic, reusable, and educational.
